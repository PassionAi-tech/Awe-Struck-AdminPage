Important before real use

  • Cloud saving uses a private, passwordless device session; no login screen is shown.
  • This device session is private. A different device needs a secure device-linking step before it can access the same workspace.
  • Keep exporting backups as an extra precaution, and do not share your device session with anyone.
  • Keep invoices and receipts; this dashboard is a planning aid, not accounting or tax advice.
